      Road data from OpenStreetMap contributors (surface, track grade, smoothness, 4WD tags). Routing and elevation by Valhalla on openstreetmap.de. Map tiles by OpenStreetMap and Esri.

      Clearance figures for preset cars are approximate. Measure your own car at its lowest point (usually the front splitter) and type it in. Mapped data can be missing or out of date, so treat a green route as a good sign, not a guarantee.

      Safety: Clearance is a planning aid. Always look at the road yourself before you drive it, never drive into water you can't see the bottom of, and turn around if a road looks worse than shown. You are responsible for where you take your car. Speed bumps and fuel from OpenStreetMap via Overpass; weather from Open-Meteo.
